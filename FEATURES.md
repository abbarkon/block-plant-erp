# Block Plant ERP - Complete Feature List

## 🎯 System Overview
This is a comprehensive, production-ready ERP system designed specifically for concrete block manufacturing plants. It handles everything from raw material procurement to finished goods sales, including HR, payroll, assets, and complete financial accounting.

- ✅ **Clear history** keeps the employee but permanently removes their attendance, payroll, leave, loans, overtime, reviews, asset assignments, documents, training, exit clearances and warnings after typing the employee code to confirm

### 6.2 Attendance System
- ✅ Daily attendance marking
- ✅ Clock in/out times
- ✅ Working hours calculation
- ✅ Attendance status (present/absent/half-day/leave)
- ✅ Date-wise attendance sheets
- ✅ Attendance cannot be recorded before an employee's joining date
- ✅ Monthly attendance reports
- ✅ Notes and remarks
- ✅ Printable written warning letters for more than two recorded working-day absences
- ✅ Warning register with period/department filters (HR editors, approvers and administrators can print)
- ✅ English and Amharic warning register and printable letter, with an EN/አማ letter toggle
- ✅ Warning letters can be revoked with an evidence/reason note; revoked letters are blocked from reprinting and can be restored
- ✅ Revocation accepts up to five evidence documents (PDF, JPG/JPEG, PNG, WEBP, DOC, DOCX; 10 MB each), stored privately and downloaded through an authenticated link
- ✅ HR users granted **Approve HR requests** can revoke and restore warnings; **Edit** access can print but cannot revoke or restore
- ✅ Separate revoked-warning panel and activity-log audit trail
- ✅ HR managers, administrators and HR letter users are notified in-app the first time a warning letter is generated, with the employee, reference, absence days and period
- ✅ Employees with a linked login account also receive their own warning notification; each warning notifies only once per set of absence dates
- ✅ Printable Amharic employee attendance sheet (**የሠራተኞች ቀሪ መለኪያ ቅጽ**) with company heading, department and Ethiopian 16–15 payroll date range
- ✅ Attendance sheet pre-fills days present, unexcused absences, late time, excused leave days and leave reasons (Annual/Casual/Sick Leave), with a choice of one page per department or all employees on one sheet
- ✅ **Export Excel** next to **Print** downloads the same sheet with the company heading, Amharic title, department, 16-15 date range, columns, legend and signatures
- ✅ A future day that already has saved attendance (e.g. tomorrow's register) counts on the sheet immediately; unrecorded future days are ignored
- ✅ Previous/current 16–15 period shortcuts, with a 30-working-day maximum per payroll month (Pagume days excluded)
- ✅ Approved paid leave counts as present and is never deducted; only approved unpaid leave is deducted from present days and payroll
- ✅ Paid leave is also counted in the **Present** figures of the attendance report, HR dashboard, department report and monthly submission summary
- ✅ Attendance statuses include **present**, **absent**, **half-day**, **leave** and **late**; late time is deducted from the days present at **8 working hours = 1 day** (2:37 late → −0.33 day)
- ✅ Company shifts are built in — morning **2:30 AM – 6:30 PM (08:30 – 12:30)** and afternoon **7:30 PM – 11:30 PM (13:30 – 17:30)** local time, with the Friday morning session ending at **5:30 AM (11:30)**
- ✅ The register records a clock-in time per employee and marks **late** automatically after **2:45 AM (08:45)** or **7:45 PM (13:45)**, showing *Late by N min*; the server applies the same rule
- ✅ **Present** is accepted as on time without a clock-in time, and **half-day** needs no time either; only **late** rows require one
- ✅ Marking a row **late** (manually or automatically from the clock-in) opens a styled popup to record the time, with a live *Late by N min / On time* result
- ✅ Each employee has a default **Work Shift** (Morning/Afternoon), shown on the employee form and list and pre-selected in the daily register
- ✅ The sheet's **በአርፋጅ የተመዘገቡበት ሰዓት** column writes the **total late time as hours:minutes** for the period (47 minutes → `0:47`, 1 h 30 min → `1:30`), summed from each late day's clock-in against the shift grace time, and that time is already deducted from **በስራ ላይ የተገኙበት ቀን** with a legend line explaining the rule
- ✅ Late is tracked in the attendance report, CSV/Excel export, monthly submission summary, department report and HR dashboard
- ✅ Amharic attendance labels use **የሰዓት መቆጣጠሪያ**
- ✅ Ethiopian calendar display (month names, weekdays and ዓ.ም.) when Amharic is selected
